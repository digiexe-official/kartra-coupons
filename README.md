## 💡How to Create Kartra Coupons

Creating and managing coupons in Kartra is a simple process. Here’s a step-by-step guide to get you started:

### 1. **Login to Your Kartra Dashboard**
Log into your Kartra account and navigate to the "Products" section. From there, click on the "Coupons" tab.

### 2. **Create a New Coupon**
Click on the "Add New Coupon" button. You’ll be prompted to provide the following details:
- **Coupon Code**: Choose a unique code that customers will use at checkout (e.g., "SAVE10" or "SUMMER25").
- **Discount Type**: Decide on the type of discount—percentage, fixed amount, or even free trials.
- **Coupon Amount**: Specify the discount value (e.g., 10% off or $20 off).
- **Usage Limit**: You can limit the number of times the coupon can be used or the number of times a customer can redeem it.

### 3. **Set Expiry Date**
Set an expiration date for your coupon to create urgency. Kartra allows you to specify a date or use the “timed” option to apply a coupon to a specific time window.

### 4. **Integrate with Your Sales Funnel**
Once your coupon is created, you can integrate it with your sales funnel or campaigns. For example, you can send a coupon to users who have abandoned their cart or offer a discount as part of an upsell offer.

### 5. **Promote Your Coupon**
Share your coupon code with your audience through email campaigns, social media, or even in paid ads. Kartra makes it easy to automate the coupon distribution process, ensuring your customers don’t miss out on the offer.
